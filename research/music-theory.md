# Music theory and music psychology: reference, not rules (2026-11-21)

Joona: "Go research some music theory ... but do not be narrowed down to music theory as sometimes you must do things that go against theory and they work ... just for more reference." Ears decide; this is what the research says, and where Aura already sits.

| Topic | What the research found | Strength | Aura today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Groove and syncopation | Medium syncopation gives the most desire to move and the most pleasure (an inverted U); too little is stiff, too much is confusing. Strongest for people who like dancing. Harmony complexity mostly changes pleasure, rhythm complexity changes both. | Peer-reviewed experiments: Witek et al., PLoS ONE 9(4) e94446 (2014); Matthews et al. (2019) | Drums are now low-syncopation (four on the floor, hats on the kick, clap on 2 and 4, open hat off-beat). The syncopation lives in the rolling bass (16ths that avoid the kick) and the arp. If the groove ever feels stiff, add syncopation in a soft, non-clicky part, not in the hats. |
| Build-up and drop | Drops give significantly higher skin conductance than the other parts of a break routine; tension rises through the build-up toward an expected drop; dancers' movement changes sharply at breakdown, build-up and drop. | Peer-reviewed: Solberg & Dibben, Music Perception (2019); Solberg & Jensenius, Empirical Musicology Review (2017); Solberg, Dancecult (2014) | Breakdown (drums out) -> 8-bar build (snare roll doubling, riser, pluck tearing open) -> half a bar of silence -> drop with everything. Matches the pattern. |
| Where tension comes from in EDM | In Top-40 EDM, form and tension/release come mostly from texture and groove, not harmony. | Peer-reviewed analysis: Osborn, Integral (2023) | One 16-bar progression throughout; energy comes from layering and the filter opening section by section. Consistent with the finding. |
| Borrowed chords (modal mixture) | A borrowed chord is brief enough that the home key is not lost; it colours rather than modulates. House often moves chords in parallel (shifting a whole chord shape). | Textbook definition; Ableton "Parallel Harmony" (practitioner) | E major (from E major) for two bars inside E minor: G# for G, the bittersweet lift, back to minor via C. Textbook mode mixture. |
| Expectation and emotion | Emotion in music comes from expectation: tension while uncertain, reward when a prediction comes true, surprise when it does not (ITPRA: imagination, tension, prediction, reaction, appraisal). | Theory with experimental grounding, contested universality: Huron, Sweet Anticipation (MIT Press, 2006) | The loop teaches the ear the progression; the borrowed E major is a small, safe surprise each pass; the silence before the drop delays a strongly predicted event. |

## Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study found on modal mixture specifically in progressive house; candidates to read: Harding, PhD thesis on harmony in dance music (Salford, 2024); Faraldo, Herrera, Jordà, tonality in EDM corpus study (Euromac 2017).
- No research number for "how loud a hi-hat should be"; Joona's sensitivity to repeated ticks is the standard for this catalog.

## Going against theory on purpose
Hats on the kick break the house convention (off-beat hats). Joona chose it by ear; it stays. The research above explains what each convention does, so breaking one is a choice, not an accident.
